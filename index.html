Service 05

CED Coating Plants & Machinery

What is CED?

CED (Cathodic Electro Deposition) is an industrial coating process in which electrically charged paint particles are deposited onto a conductive component to provide an even protective coating.

What we do:

  • CED plant design
  • CED plant fabrication
  • Plant installation
  • CED machinery and equipment
  • Conveyor and material handling systems
  • Process tank and supporting systems
  • Electrical and control support
  • Testing and commissioning support
  • Maintenance and servicing

Why is it important?

CED coating can provide consistent coating coverage and is widely used where controlled and uniform protective coating is required.

How SS Industries helps:

SS Industries provides CED plant and machinery solutions based on project requirements, production needs and site conditions.
